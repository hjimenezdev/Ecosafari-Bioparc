EcoSafari Bioparc

About

EcoSafari Bioparc is a unique wildlife park that combines the experience of a safari with the excitement of a zoo. Visitors can explore the park's expansive grounds by foot or by car, observing exotic animals from around the world in spacious enclosures that mimic their natural habitats. The park also offers educational programs for children and adults alike, as well as behind-the-scenes tours that provide a glimpse into the daily lives of its animal residents. EcoSafari Bioparc is committed to conservation and wildlife preservation, and it is actively involved in breeding programs for endangered species and habitat restoration projects. The park's mission is to inspire peopleto appreciate and protect the diversity of life on our planet.


The biopark's dedication to showcasing Iberian and Mediterranean wildlife is evident in its rich diversity. From the elusive Iberian lynx to the magnificent Spanish imperial eagle, native species are presented in carefully crafted habitats that authentically mimic their natural environments. Visitors have the opportunity to observe and connect with these animals, fostering an appreciation for the local biodiversity.

General rules

1. Respect Wildlife and our Animals: Do not attempt to approach, feed, touch, tease or do any act to cause disturbance, annoyance, or injury to any wildlife or our animals. Maintain a safe distance and appreciate them from afar.

2. No smoking: Smoking is not permitted anywhere in the park. This includes all indoor and outdoor areas, including the parking lot. Smoking is only permitted in designated smoking areas outside the park's main entrance.

3. No photography: Photography is not permitted anywhere in the park. This includes all indoor and outdoor areas, including the parking lot. Photography is only permitted in designated areas outside the park's main entrance.

4. No Bicycles, Tricycles, Skateboards, Scooters, Rollerblades, or Skates: Usage of the above items is prohibited in our Parks. Cyclists must park at designated bike parking areas and enjoy our Parks on foot.

5. No Open Fire: All forms of open fire, including BBQs and lit candles, are prohibited. Additionally, strict compliance with this regulation is required by all residents and visitors to ensure the safety of the community.

6. No balloons in the Ticketed Areas: Balloons are not permitted in the Ticketed Areas, in the event of disturbance, annoyance, or safety concerns to the animals resulting from a balloon bursting.

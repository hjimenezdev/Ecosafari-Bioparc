Facilities

The biopark is a marvel of modern conservation, boasting state-of-the-art facilities that provide a unique and immersive experience for visitors. As you enter the park, you'll be greeted by lush landscapes that replicate the natural habitats of the diverse species housed within. The enclosures, designed with a focus on animal welfare and environmental enrichment, showcase an array of flora and fauna in a harmonious balance.


One of the highlight features is the research center, where scientists and conservationists collaborate to study and protect endangered species. Visitors can witness firsthand the groundbreaking work being done to ensure the survival of these threatened animals. The educational facilities are equally impressive, providing interactive exhibits and informative sessions that raise awareness about biodiversity and the importance of conservation.


Moreover, the biopark emphasizes sustainable practices in its operations, employing cutting-edge technology for waste management, energy conservation, and eco-friendly construction. The commitment to environmental stewardship extends to every aspect of the facility, making it not only a captivating destination but also a model for responsible and ethical wildlife conservation.

African forest

Primate Paradise

Welcome to the enchanting African Forest habitat at our zoo, where an immersive experience awaits you beneath the towering canopy. As you step into this lush environment, you'll be surrounded by an array of diverse flora and majestic fauna, all meticulously designed to replicate the rich ecosystems found in the heart of Africa.

Savannah Serenity

The centerpiece of the African Forest is the expansive canopy walkway, allowing visitors a unique perspective as they stroll amidst the treetops. From this elevated vantage point, you can observe the natural behaviors of our resident wildlife, including playful primates swinging from branch to branch and colorful birds gracefully gliding through the air.

Canopy Heights

This habitat is home to a variety of endangered species, each playing a crucial role in the delicate balance of the ecosystem. The conservation efforts here are paramount, with dedicated research facilities where scientists study the behavior, reproduction, and overall well-being of these remarkable creatures, contributing valuable insights to global conservation initiatives.

Research Oasis

Our commitment to education is reflected in the interpretive exhibits scattered throughout the African Forest, providing informative displays about the importance of preserving these ecosystems and the challenges faced by the species within. The interactive learning opportunities ensure that visitors of all ages leave with a heightened awareness of the need for wildlife protection.

Avian Haven

The landscape architecture of the African Forest is carefully curated to mirror the varied terrain found in Africa, from dense rainforests to open savannahs. The result is a visually stunning and ecologically sound habitat that serves as a beacon of our dedication to the well-being of the animals that call it home

Conservation Corner

Visitors can also participate in unique behind-the-scenes tours that offer a glimpse into the daily lives of the caretakers and the efforts taken to ensure the physical and mental health of the animals. These experiences foster a deeper connection between guests and the wildlife, fostering a sense of responsibility for their conservation.

Colossal mountains

Snow Leopard Sanctuary

Welcome to the breathtaking Colossal Mountains habitat, a majestic realm within our zoo that transports visitors to the heights of towering peaks and the depths of lush valleys. This immersive exhibit showcases the diverse and resilient species that call mountainous regions home, offering a glimpse into the unique challenges and wonders of these elevated ecosystems.

Alpine Aviary

The heart of the Colossal Mountains is a meticulously crafted landscape that mirrors the rugged terrain found in mountain ranges around the world. As you explore, you'll encounter stunning rock formations, cascading waterfalls, and meandering streams that create a naturalistic haven for a variety of wildlife, from elusive snow leopards to agile mountain goats.

Summit Overlook

One of the highlights of this habitat is the Alpine Adventure Trail, a winding path that takes visitors on a journey through different elevations, allowing them to witness the gradual changes in vegetation and climate. Educational signage along the trail provides insights into the adaptability of the flora and the remarkable survival strategies employed by the animals that thrive in these high-altitude environments.

Mountain Streams Exhibit

The Conservation Chalet stands as a hub for ongoing research initiatives focused on mountainous ecosystems. Scientists at the chalet study the impact of climate change on alpine flora and fauna, contributing valuable data to global conservation efforts. Visitors can engage with interactive displays that illustrate the interconnectedness of mountain ecosystems and the importance of preserving these fragile habitats.

Rocky Ridge Trail

For a more immersive experience, the Colossal Mountains habitat includes a Mountain Aviary where visitors can marvel at the aerial acrobatics of alpine birds, including majestic eagles and colorful ptarmigans. Regular bird shows and feeding sessions provide entertainment while highlighting the crucial role these birds play in maintaining ecological balance.

Highland Flora Garden

As part of our commitment to education, the habitat features a dedicated Mountain Discovery Center, where interactive exhibits and engaging demonstrations deepen visitors' understanding of the geological processes that shape mountains and the role they play in sustaining life on Earth.

Artic Ring of Life

Frozen Tundra Territory

Welcome to the mesmerizing Arctic Ring of Life, an extraordinary habitat within our zoo that transports visitors to the frozen wonders of the far north. This immersive exhibit is designed to replicate the harsh yet beautiful conditions of the Arctic, providing a unique opportunity to witness the incredible species that thrive in this icy realm.

Polar Bear Cove

The Penguin Paradise within the Arctic Ring of Life features a specially designed enclosure for several species of adorable penguins, where they can frolic in the snow, swim in icy waters, and delight visitors with their playful antics. Interactive feeding sessions and scheduled educational programs provide a deeper understanding of these charming Arctic residents.

Arctic Waters Exhibition

The heart of the Arctic Ring of Life is a vast, chilled pool that mimics the Arctic Ocean, complete with floating icebergs and crystal-clear waters. Here, guests can marvel at the graceful movements of powerful marine mammals such as seals and polar bears, as they navigate the chilly waters and showcase their incredible adaptability to extreme cold.

Aurora Borealis Theater

One of the focal points of this habitat is the Igloo Observatory, a unique structure that allows visitors to observe the underwater antics of Arctic species through large, transparent walls. Educational displays within the observatory provide insights into the conservation challenges faced by these remarkable animals and the zoo's commitment to their well-being.

Penguin Wonderland

The Northern Lights Theater offers a multisensory experience, simulating the dazzling colors and patterns of the Aurora Borealis. This captivating feature provides a backdrop for educational presentations on Arctic climate change, showcasing the importance of global efforts to preserve the delicate balance of this unique polar environment.

Iceberg Observation Outpost

As part of our commitment to education and conservation, the Arctic Ring of Life includes a Research Station where scientists conduct studies on the impact of climate change on Arctic ecosystems. Visitors can engage with ongoing research projects, gaining valuable insights into the challenges faced by polar species and the measures being taken to ensure their survival.
